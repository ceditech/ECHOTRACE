# Proposed evidence review workflow

Initial fields are **NOT REQUESTED / NOT SUPPLIED / NOT REVIEWED**. Owner-supplied documents can enter at SUPPLIED once receipt is logged under an authorized process. Identifying possible evidence does not change any status.

1. Identify evidence and map it to request and asset IDs.
2. Log supplied record and custody metadata.
3. Match source and exact asset/version.
4. Assess issuer authority and applicability.
5. Check commercial game use.
6. Check derivative and composite scope.
7. Record restrictions and attribution.
8. Route unresolved questions to qualified review where needed.
9. Record authorized acceptance or rejection with scope and reasons.
10. Seek separate authority for any governance update.

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| NOT REQUESTED | Initial planning state. No authorized request has been issued. |
| REQUESTED | An authorized request was actually issued and logged. |
| SUPPLIED | A specific evidence record was actually received and logged. |
| INCOMPLETE | Material fields, pages, issuer or linkage are missing. |
| UNDER REVIEW | A designated reviewer is assessing the actual submission. |
| NEEDS QUALIFIED REVIEW | Applicability, authority or terms need qualified assessment. |
| ACCEPTED AS EVIDENCE | Authorized reviewer recorded sufficient evidence for a specified purpose and scope. |
| REJECTED AS INSUFFICIENT | Authorized reviewer recorded the unresolved deficiency and reason. |

INCOMPLETE returns to SUPPLIED when a revised record arrives. NEEDS QUALIFIED REVIEW returns to UNDER REVIEW when the needed assessment is available, or to INCOMPLETE if material is missing. Preserve the disposition and create a new review record for a superseding submission. An acceptance record identifies the exact evidence and asset IDs, applicability and reviewed uses, restrictions, open questions, reviewer authority, date and rationale. Rejection records the deficiency and what could address it.

ACCEPTED AS EVIDENCE is a scoped review disposition. Legal clearance, gate changes and production approval each require separate authority. The owner or designated reviewer would record a decision only under a later authorization.
