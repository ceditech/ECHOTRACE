# Proposed evidence submission checklist

Future intake requires a separate owner authorization covering the workstreams, permitted methods, location and access roles. The following procedure describes a potential owner-supplied submission.

1. Supply an issuer-original PDF or text license, written grant or assignment, acquisition record linked to applicable terms, or original creation/export record. A dated export with context can support identity or authenticity. A screenshot or isolated excerpt is supporting evidence where issuer, version or surrounding terms still need verification.
2. Assign an evidence ID to each record. Identify the request ID, every covered asset ID, exact audited repository path and SHA-256, original source or item identifier and the record's issuer. Reference the same evidence ID across assets and derivatives when the reviewed scope covers them.
3. Supply applicable version, effective date, acquisition or generation context, and an explanation of why the document applies. Record authenticity support such as issuer details, signatures where available, matching identifiers or contemporaneous records. Record any uncertainty.
4. Specify commercial game distribution, applicable modification and derivative use, distribution/embedding, attribution and restrictions, including platform, territory, time and transfer terms where stated. Record silence or missing proof as NOT ESTABLISHED. Any applicability exclusion requires a reason tied to the intended use.
5. Supply a minimally redacted review summary and retain the complete record in approved private custody. Preserve clauses and context needed for review. Track the record version, digest where permitted, custody reference and redactions.
6. Log the proposed review disposition with reviewer role, date, evidence IDs, assessed scope, reasons and unresolved questions. Until actual review, retain the corresponding initial or intermediate status. Route missing information to INCOMPLETE and material applicability questions to NEEDS QUALIFIED REVIEW.

Use the ten-point checklist under each of the seven P0 bundles. Identification of audited assets is established through REV-001. Coverage of every required upstream input and every permission criterion awaits evidence. Forms of proof vary with origin and applicable terms, and a verified shared grant may serve several items.
