## Seating, support and association

- Mara's head/torso/lap read at the window-side chair; existing traced chair-back occlusion hides the lower figure. She is low in the frame relative to the large table and remains visually small. This can be a stylized depth choice or a staging defect; absent 3D/chair measurements, owner acceptance is needed.
- Owen's bent legs/visible shoe and table-edge occlusion suggest seating, but he lies between table silhouettes and may read as generic central lounge seating. Moving a clue nearer him is not a substitute for clear physical table association. No new gaze/gesture/custody clue is invented.
- Both figures retain warm window light; stylistic sharpness and clothing brightness differ from distant background people. No tonal retouch or synthetic support shadow is added.
- Uniform scene zoom cannot change person/table ratios. Uncontrolled rescaling would change chair contact, depth, head clearance and clue/landmark occlusion; it is not a justified correction from one comment.
