# Facilitator script — do not show participants

Before Start, say exactly:

“Look carefully at the scene. Try to remember the objects, table numbers and where things are located.”

Do not disclose the number or nature of expected changes. During original and blank phases, remain silent. At changed-scene onset ask exactly:

“What changed?”

Do not confirm correctness, point, zoom, annotate, suggest a location or ask about missed changes. Record responses verbatim with elapsed seconds. If finished, use End recall; otherwise the screen blanks at 60 seconds. Lock free-recall scores.

Only after locking, ask:

- “What did you think the small document on the left table was?” Record passport recognition separately from disappearance detection.
- “How did you understand the table numbers in the two scenes?” Record exchange versus merely different numerals.
- “What did the brown item look like, and where was it in each scene?” Then, if needed, ask about its emblem and strap; mark each prompted recognition answer accordingly.
- “Did you see two distinct people? How would you describe them and their locations?” Do not expect names that were never introduced. Facilitator maps descriptions to Mara and Owen.
- “Which seating or table did each person seem associated with?” Record window-side Mara and plant-side Owen association independently.
- “Did either person seem to move or change?” A reported character change is a false positive, never a fourth authored change.
- “Did the people distract you from anything else? Was anything hard to recognize?”

Do not explain the solution/custody story before any optional familiarized-size trial. Keep all follow-up answers separate from independent evidence.
