# Facilitator-only scoring rubric

Score each of the three changes independently from pre-lock free recall only. Use IDENTIFIED, PARTIAL or MISSED. FALSE POSITIVE codes additional claims of changes that did not occur; it is not a replacement for the three independent scores. Keep a verbatim false-positive list and count, including claims that characters moved.

| Change | IDENTIFIED | PARTIAL | MISSED |
|---|---|---|---|
| Passport removal | Identifies passport/document booklet and disappearance from window-side table/mat | “Something disappeared” or vague book/document with incomplete meaningful location/identity | No meaningful disappearance identification |
| Stand exchange | Describes 12 and 21 switching tables or equivalent reversed left/right table association | Notices different numerals without understanding exchange | No meaningful stand difference |
| Wallet movement | Identifies same wallet/pouch moving from window-side/left to plant-side/right table | Notices brown item in different place without clear identity or source/destination | No meaningful movement identification |

An unequivocal visual description of the passport booklet can support event identification without knowing the word passport. Record explicit passport recognition separately (YES/NO/UNCERTAIN); do not equate generic object disappearance with passport recognition. Star emblem and striped strap recognition are separate fields and are not required for wallet-movement credit. Numeral recognition alone does not establish exchange. No exact terminology or title reading is required.

For each change, record first meaningful response latency from recall onset, uncertainty and verbatim statement. Mark missing latency blank, never zero. Post-lock prompted recognition receives no independent credit. If ambiguity remains, retain PARTIAL and explain; do not infer an unstated relationship. Distinct character recognition does not require names. Record seating association, perceived movement and distraction separately; characters are stable across snapshots.

Suggested review trigger (not a new product acceptance rule): any missed change, recurring partial recognition, false movement report or strong distraction merits focused owner review. Three viewers supply observations, not statistically validated success rates. Owner decides next steps; testing approval is not production approval.
