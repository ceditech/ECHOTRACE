# Next owner actions

Review the proposed first batch and decide whether suitable records already held by the owner justify a separate review. If so, explicitly identify the exact documents, approved location, authorized reviewer, applicable request and asset IDs, read-only methods, confidentiality controls, allowed report outputs, criteria and stop conditions. Use the later authorization to establish how approved access would occur. A confidential source should remain in approved private custody.

If no suitable record is available or the scope is undecided, keep review dormant. D05 rights resolution and the adoption hold remain open. Any subsequent qualified review, governance update or production decision requires its own authority.
