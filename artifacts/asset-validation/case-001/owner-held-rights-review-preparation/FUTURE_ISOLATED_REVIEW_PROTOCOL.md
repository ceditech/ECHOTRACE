# Future isolated review protocol

**Non-executable proposal.** The owner would have to explicitly authorize the following fields before a review begins.

- Exact documents supplied
- Approved access location
- Authorized reviewer
- Affected request bundles
- Asset categories covered
- Permitted read-only actions
- Confidentiality and storage rules
- Allowed report outputs
- Review criteria
- Stop conditions

Access would be confined to the named documents and approved location. Use read-only review with no copying or redistribution unless a distinct scope permits it. Repository visibility alone supplies no copying authority. Log actual receipt only under that later authorization. Limit each finding to exact asset and use, record omissions and keep an auditable disposition. Route unresolved applicability or conflict to the owner or authorized qualified reviewer. No external research or provider contact is part of the protocol.

| Proposed classification | Meaning |
|---|---|
| NOT SUPPLIED | Initial state. No record received. |
| SUPPLIED — NOT REVIEWED | Actual authorized receipt logged. |
| INCOMPLETE | Required context, linkage or coverage is missing. |
| UNDER REVIEW | Designated reviewer is examining a supplied record. |
| EVIDENCE SUPPORTS SPECIFIED USE | Reviewer records support tied to exact asset, use and applicable restrictions. |
| EVIDENCE DOES NOT ESTABLISH SPECIFIED USE | Record fails to establish the reviewed use or remains silent on it. |
| CONFLICTING EVIDENCE | Material sources disagree and require reconciliation. |
| NEEDS QUALIFIED REVIEW | An authorized qualified reviewer must assess unresolved rights or applicability questions. |

Review criteria

- Exact covered asset and SHA-256/version
- Issuer and authority
- Authenticity, applicable source, effective date and context
- Commercial game use
- Modification and derivative use
- Distribution and embedding, distinguishing raster and font-file use
- Restrictions and attribution
- Unresolved inherited composite dependencies
- Need for qualified review and recorded scope of disposition

Stop conditions

- Missing or ambiguous authorization, document or location
- Unapproved copy, transfer, contact, external research or scope expansion needed
- Confidentiality cannot be maintained
- Mismatch, incomplete evidence or conflict blocks the proposed finding
- Legal clearance or production adoption requested outside review authority

Evidence support is scoped to the specific use reviewed. Legal clearance and production approval require distinct authority. [Machine protocol](future-review-protocol.json)
